About StudentHub

StudentHub is a simple online student portal for CHARUSAT University.

It helps students stay updated about college events and activities from one place.

What StudentHub Offers

  • Student registration and login
  • Upcoming college events
  • Student dashboard
  • Profile management
  • College announcements
  • Student support

Our Objectives

  • Make college information easy to access.
  • Keep students updated about events.
  • Provide a simple student portal.
  • Improve communication between students and college.

How StudentHub Works

  1. Create your StudentHub account.
  2. Login to the student portal.
  3. Check events and announcements.
  4. Manage your student profile.
