Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about StudentHub.

1. How can I create a StudentHub account?

Go to the Register page, enter your details and click Create Account.

2. How do I login?

Open the Login page and enter your username and password.

3. Where can I see college events?

All upcoming college events are available on the Events page.

4. Can I update my profile?

Yes. Open the Profile page and update your personal or academic information.

5. How can I contact StudentHub?

Visit the Contact page and send your query using the contact form.

6. Who can access the Admin page?

The Admin page is intended for authorized college staff.
