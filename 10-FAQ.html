Frequently Asked Questions

1. How can I create a StudentHub account?

Open the Register page, enter your details and click the Create Account button.

2. How do I login to StudentHub?

Go to the Login page and enter your username and password.

3. Where can I view upcoming events?

All college events are available on the Events page.

4. Can I update my profile information?

Yes. Open the Profile page and update your personal or academic details.

5. How can I contact the StudentHub team?

Visit the Contact page and submit your query using the contact form.

6. Who can access the Admin page?

Only authorized college staff members are allowed to access the Admin Panel.

7. Do I need to register for every event?

Yes. Students should register separately for each event they wish to participate in.
